\section{File header}
The file header shall bear the following data
\begin{table}[H]
   \caption{File header} 
   \label{tab:HEADER}
   \small
   %\centering
   \begin{tabular}{|l|r|r|r|}
   %\midrule
   \textbf{Field} & \textbf{Type} & \textbf{Size in bytes} & \textbf{Notes} \\ 
   ICD version & 2 chars string & 2 & \strpad\\
   Effective date & unsigned int & 4 & \DateType \\
   Expiration date & unsigned int & 4 & \DateType \\
   CRC32 & unsigned integer & 4& \\
   Database Name & 30 chars string & 30 & \strpad\\
   Number of tables & unsigned char & 1 & \\
   \end{tabular}
\end{table}
